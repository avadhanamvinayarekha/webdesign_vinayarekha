Am I Eligible?

Yes, you are eligible to donate blood if you meet the following conditions:

  • You are above 17 years of age
  • Your weight is more than 50 kg
  • You are in good health
    (If you are on medication or have any health concerns, please contact us and we will help you find out if you can donate.)
