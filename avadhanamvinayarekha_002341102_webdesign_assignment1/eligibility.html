Am I Eligible?

Yes, you are eligible to donate blood if you meet the following conditions:

  • You are above 17 years of age
  • Your weight is more than 50 kg
  • You are in good health
    (If you are on medication or have any health concerns, please contact us and we will help you find out if you can donate.)

Before you donate

What should I eat?

Eat iron-rich foods and stay hydrated before donating blood.

What should I avoid?

Avoid alcohol and fatty foods for at least 24 hours before your donation.

How often is inventory updated?

Our system refreshes blood stock data every 24 hours.
