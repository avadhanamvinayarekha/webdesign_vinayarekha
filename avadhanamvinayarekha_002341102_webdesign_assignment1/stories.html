Recipient Story: Molly

Molly is a 7-year-old busy, happy little girl who takes great joy in the small things in life. She has beta thalassemia major, a condition where the body doesn’t make enough healthy hemoglobin, making her transfusion-dependent for life.

“When I get new blood, I feel better,” Molly says. Her mother shares: “Without blood donations, Molly would not survive. Thanks to the blood donation bank, my daughter gets to live.”

As Molly’s mom, I donate blood every 6 months to the blood donation bank—because every two seconds, someone like Molly needs blood.

My name is Andrew Treen Moon. My wife Angie gave birth to three identical triplet girls, and unexpected complications arose where she needed over 50 units of blood.

If it weren’t for the blood donation bank, Angie would not be here to share her story today.

As her husband, I make sure to donate blood regularly to the blood donation bank. Blood donations truly save and impact people’s lives.
